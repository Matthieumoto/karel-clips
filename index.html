karel-clips

karel-clips is a personal desktop tool used by the streamer karel_vdc. It turns highlights from his own Twitch live streams into short vertical videos with subtitles, then publishes them to his own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, after he reviews and approves each clip manually.

The tool runs only on the creator's own computer. It is not offered to other users and it does not access anyone else's account.

Outil personnel du streamer karel_vdc : il découpe ses propres lives Twitch en clips verticaux sous-titrés et les publie sur ses propres comptes, après validation manuelle.
