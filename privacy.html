Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 19, 2026

1. What data is used

  • The owner's own Twitch stream recordings, processed locally on his computer.
  • Access tokens for the owner's own TikTok, YouTube and Instagram accounts, used only to publish his videos.
  • From TikTok: basic account information (user.info.basic) and the right to publish videos (video.publish).

2. Where data is stored

Everything stays on the owner's computer. Tokens are stored in a private file that only the owner can read. No data is sent to any server other than the official TikTok, YouTube and Instagram APIs.

3. Sharing

No data is sold, shared or used for advertising. No data from other TikTok users is collected.

4. Deleting data

The owner can disconnect the app at any time in the TikTok settings (Security → Manage app permissions) and delete the local token files.
